How to donate

Choose the method that suits you. Any of the three can be used.

1

Offline: visit the school

Visit the school in person at Ward No. 3, Bamori, Kota Road, Anta, District Baran, Rajasthan, and contribute directly.

  • Call the school office on the number shown on the About page before you visit.
  • Ask the office for the receipt procedure.
2

Online: Gyansankalp Portal

Donate through the official Gyansankalp Portal.

Donate on Gyansankalp Portal

The official link will be added here once it has been verified. No link has been added yet.

3

Direct QR payment

Scan the school's official donation QR code with a payment app that supports QR scanning.

Before you pay, check that the name shown on the payment screen matches MAHATMA GANDHI GOVT. SCHOOL ANTA.

Direct QR payment

Scan to contribute

This area shows the school's official donation QR code.

  1. Open your payment app and choose the QR scan option.
  2. Scan the code shown here.
  3. Check the name on the payment screen matches MAHATMA GANDHI GOVT. SCHOOL ANTA, then pay.

Please note

Before you give

Use only the methods on this page. Payment details, the portal link, the QR code and donor records will be added once they have been verified by the school. Check any payment request with the school office before you pay.
