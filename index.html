School introduction

MAHATMA GANDHI GOVT. SCHOOL ANTA

A co-educational government school in Ward No. 03, Nagar Palika Anta, District Baran, offering education from Nursery to Class 12. Instruction is in Hindi at the primary level and English at the secondary level, with Sanskrit as the third language.
