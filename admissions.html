Admissions

Join Our School

Step by step

How to apply

  1. STEP 1 – Student Registration

    Register through the official Rajasthan ShalaDarpan Admission Portal. Select the desired class and enter the student's Date of Birth, Name, Father's Name and Candidate/Parent's Mobile Number. Save the Application Number received through SMS.

  2. STEP 2 – Application Form Submission

    Login using the Application Number, Candidate Name and Date of Birth. Fill in the admission application carefully, including previous school and academic details wherever required. Review the application and keep a printed copy for future reference.

  3. STEP 3 – Admission Result & Further Process

    Check the admission result through the official ShalaDarpan portal using your login credentials. Follow the official admission schedule and school notices for selection, document verification and completion of admission formalities.

E

Eligibility

Admission is available for the classes listed in the official ShalaDarpan Admission Portal, subject to school availability and applicable admission guidelines. Applicants may include students from Government Schools, Private Schools, Other State Schools and first-time learners. Age-based admission options are also provided for eligible students in Classes 2–8.

D

Documents required

Parents should keep the student's Date of Birth details, previous school information and available academic records ready. Additional documents may be required depending on the student's admission category and the applicable school admission guidelines. Please contact the school office for specific requirements.

T

Important dates

Admission registration dates, application deadlines and other important updates will be published as per the official Rajasthan ShalaDarpan admission schedule. Parents are advised to check this page and the official portal regularly for the latest announcements.

Enquiries

Talk to the School Office

Contact the school office for admission enquiries, class availability, eligibility and document-related assistance.
