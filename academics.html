Curriculum

Classes run from Nursery to Class 12. Instruction is in English at both the primary and secondary levels. Sanskrit is the third language.

The Higher Secondary Science stream offers Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics and Physics. Computer subject options listed on the school record are Computer Science, Informatics Practice and Multimedia and Web Technology.
