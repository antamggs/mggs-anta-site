F · Other special eligibility

Palanhar Yojana

पालनहार योजना

Monthly support for orphaned children and children in other listed difficult circumstances, run by the Social Justice and Empowerment Department.

Who may benefit
Children under 18 in the categories listed in the 2022 rules, such as orphans, children of a parent with HIV/AIDS, leprosy or a disability of 40% or more, and children of certain widowed, divorced or deserted mothers. Rajasthan residence is required.
Main benefit (2022 rules)
Orphans: ₹1,500 per month (age 0–6) and ₹2,500 per month (age 6–18). Other listed categories: ₹500 per month (age 0–6) and ₹1,000 per month (age 6–18). Yearly lump sum of ₹2,000 for books, clothes and shoes, except for the widow and step-mother categories.

At this school: Conditional on category and residence.

Verified under the 2022 rules · 2026-27 amounts: to be confirmed

Palanhar 2022 rules (PDF)
